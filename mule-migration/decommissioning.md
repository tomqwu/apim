# Decommissioning checklist

- No traffic or scheduled execution for the approved observation period.
- Consumers, credentials, certificates, routes, DNS, firewall rules, queues/topics, files, databases, connectors, and shared-domain dependencies reconciled.
- Audit, logs, source, configuration, test evidence, operational records, and retention obligations archived.
- Business and technical owners approve behavior and financial closure.
- Monitoring and support records closed; licenses/infrastructure adjusted.
- Recovery is documented for the required retention period, then sensitive data is securely deleted.
