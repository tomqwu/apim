No comparative ranking or observed production-fit result is published. Stakeholders have selected Kong as the planning direction, and self-managed hybrid `KP-SMH1` is the leading bounded deployment target. That priority comes from business direction, not the still-unpopulated criterion ledger. See `research/findings.md` for confirmed facts, `docs/00-executive-summary.md` for the current decision posture, and `docs/47-kong-enterprise-platform-strategy.md` for the outcome contract.

Gate 4 migration-at-scale approval additionally requires representative E4 production-pilot results.
