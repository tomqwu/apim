# Product comparison and decision

## Participants

Architecture board, security, operations, procurement, finance, vendors.

## Preparation

- Answer or assign Q-161–Q-180 from `question-bank.md`.
- Bring current diagrams, inventory, runbooks, metrics, incident evidence, and relevant policy/contract extracts.
- Mark confidential artifacts by location; do not copy secrets or customer data into this repository.

## 120-minute agenda

1. Outcomes, scope, and assumptions — 10 minutes.
2. Evidence-led current state or vendor architecture — 25 minutes.
3. Organization-specific scenarios and failure paths — 35 minutes.
4. Requirements, gaps, and trade-offs — 30 minutes.
5. Decisions, actions, owners, and due dates — 20 minutes.

## Required outputs

Frozen evidence gaps; gate status; TCO inputs; decision schedule.

## Exit rule

Unanswered decision-critical questions become explicit matrix unknowns with owners and due dates; they are not resolved by consensus language.
