# Open questions

## Decision-critical organizational inputs

1. What does PCF mean in the current estate, and what are its retirement milestones?
2. Which Azure regions, private locations, and other clouds must host data planes?
3. What data may leave approved regions or enterprise-controlled networks as configuration, metadata, analytics, logs, or support bundles?
4. What are request-path, change-plane, portal, and analytics SLO/RTO/RPO targets?
5. Which Mule workloads and connectors are business critical, stateful, or unsupported?
6. Which external/internal/partner APIs and protocols represent the product choice?
7. Which identity, PKI, secrets, SIEM/APM, WAF, DNS, and network controls are mandatory?
8. What federation/tenancy model and segregation of duties are required?
9. What licensed capabilities, support terms, and commercial ceilings apply?
10. What staffing and on-call model can the organization sustain for self-managed runtime/control components?

The complete 180-question bank is in `workshops/question-bank.md`; owners and due dates are assigned during workshop preparation.
