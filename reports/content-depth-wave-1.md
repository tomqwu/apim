## Principal verdict

The repository has moved from an outline-heavy publication shell to a substantive, governed content baseline. The baseline review found a median numbered-document length of 148 words, 32 of 41 numbered documents below 300 words, no inline figures or point-of-claim external sources in docs 10–32, 647 words across the eight comparison pages, and 35–39-word vendor research stubs. The release candidate now contains 42 contract-enforced principal studies with 165,731 words, three decision-grade protocols with 10,397 words, deep symmetric dossiers, a synthetic enterprise case, public failure cases, a canonical top-ten industry-problem taxonomy, a vendor-neutral industry-practice and realistic-case system, a Kong multicloud study roadmap, and one visible figure contract for every inline Mermaid.

That is a material editorial and methodological improvement. It is **not** product evidence. All 120 criteria remain unknown, the seven entries remain bounded archetypes rather than exact purchasable options, and no finalist has completed the 28 atomic E3 protocol cases. The defensible decision remains: accept the reusable evidence system for continued work, approve Gate 0 if organizational authority and inputs are available, and do not select a platform.

## Benchmark lessons applied

The baseline review used [`tomqwu/aml_open_framework`](https://github.com/tomqwu/aml_open_framework/tree/4a7a8d573ba1369d1e8513526ee905938a29b3d3) as a structural benchmark, not a content, domain or branding template. The transferable pattern was:

> Decision problem → sourced argument → named audience → concrete artifact → reproducible proof → interpreted result → limitation → decision or next gate

The release candidate applies that pattern through answer-first studies, reference-case journey/incident IDs, causal mechanism and failure analysis, point-of-use official sources, inline exhibits, testable falsifiers, result-bundle contracts and explicit next gates. It deliberately does not copy repository volume, progress-log sprawl, screenshots as proof, promotional certainty, AML domain assumptions or visual identity.

## Systemic remediation completed in the release candidate

### 1. Deep, symmetric study corpus

Docs 00–36 and 41–45 now use the principal-study contract. Kong, Azure API Management, Apigee, MuleSoft and secondary-candidate material uses bounded archetypes, topology/state/ownership analysis, failure modes, counter-evidence, non-fit conditions, official sources, proof plans and Gate-1 blockers. Docs 25–31 use one comparative frame rather than candidate-specific checklists. Doc 43 adds a canonical P1–P10 problem portfolio across vendors; doc 45 translates it into proposed operating practices, eight synthetic practice cases, anti-patterns and evidence gates; doc 44 projects it into bounded Kong operating models and evidence workstreams without creating a second taxonomy or conferring execution priority.

### 2. Real-world decision context

[RE-1](../docs/41-enterprise-reference-case.md) supplies six critical journeys and eight compound incidents spanning ambiguous money movement, stale configuration, PKI rollover, noisy neighbours, telemetry backpressure, regional authority, schema drift and irreversible migration effects. The [public failure casebook](../docs/42-public-failure-casebook.md) converts five primary-source incident cases into symmetric test implications without claiming product equivalence.

### 3. Evidence-bounded option and scoring method

The seven candidates are now explicitly bounded archetypes. Edition, version, topology, region, entitlement, plugin, support and responsibility fields must close before a row becomes an exact scoreable option. The scoring method now requires mandatory gates first, a common-evidence comparison, category coverage, full-weight lower/upper bounds, maximum regret and missing-not-at-random sensitivity. An option-specific normalized score over a selective evidence set is descriptive only.

### 4. Atomic hard-gated proof

The PoC separates 16 aggregate status-register items from 28 atomic deep-protocol cases. Every applicable real-world case is pass/fail/indeterminate before a secondary preference score exists. A certificate, isolation, telemetry, schema, capacity or migration failure cannot disappear inside an average. Business idempotency and outcome truth are owned by the transfer domain; the gateway propagates the key and does not manufacture unsafe retries.

### 5. Article-owned visual reasoning

Each inline Mermaid is adjacent to a stable unique ID/title, depicted scope, excluded scope, source/evidence/as-of field, accessible equivalent, interpretation and limitation. The validator checks exact cardinality, order, ISO date and repository-wide ID uniqueness. The Visual Atlas and presentations remain indexes/projections of article-owned arguments rather than substitute analysis.

### 6. Source-promotion boundary

The [source-coverage report](source-coverage.md) and [machine-readable usage ledger](source-coverage.csv) scan 68 article files and fail CI when citation state drifts. Registered citations may support decision-bearing findings only through the finding chain; 227 contextual URLs remain explanation-only until an evidence owner promotes the claims that can change a gate, score or recommendation.

### 7. One dependency-aware roadmap

The [repository roadmap](../docs/39-repository-roadmap.md) now marks the principal content baseline as implemented with independent acceptance open, not “next.” It uses the same Gate 0–5 sequence as the [delivery roadmap](../docs/36-implementation-roadmap.md), separates elapsed-time scenarios from role/capacity effort, and removes the unsupported promise of Gate-2 closure in 90 days.

### 8. Neutral audience and presentation story

Six role paths retain one evidence base. The generic platform-team path is symmetric across Kong, APIM, Apigee and MuleSoft. Kong is a named falsifiable sequencing hypothesis with no execution priority. The overview and presentation begin with decision state, not content volume or a presumed finalist.

## Publication and decision gates

- **Repository publication:** may proceed only after final independent release QA records no unresolved P0/P1 publication defect and all deterministic checks pass on the release tree.
- **Principal-content acceptance:** remains open until independent reviewers accept the applicable PCR exit evidence. Implementation and validator success are necessary but not sufficient.
- **Gate 0:** may be requested; it authorizes evidence closure, not a candidate or 90-day Gate-2 promise.
- **Gate 1–2:** remain blocked by exact option resolution, organization inputs, mandatory conditions, equivalent E1/E2 coverage, E3 execution, TCO/support and uncertainty analysis.
- **Gate 3–5:** remain blocked by foundation readiness, representative E4 pilots, accepted patterns and dependency-zero evidence.
