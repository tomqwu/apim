## Content maturity boundary

The delivered portal, presentation routes, source manifest, visual rendering, and validation workflow establish a technical publication baseline only. Portal completion does not imply that every page is a substantive study, that product coverage is symmetric, that evidence is decision-grade, or that a recommendation is ready. Editorial and comparative completion are governed by the [principal content review](content-research-principal-review.md), its [remediation backlog](content-remediation-backlog.csv), and the [repository roadmap](../docs/39-repository-roadmap.md).

## Required organization-specific inputs before recommendation

Mule/PCF/AKS inventory, representative APIs and traffic, SLO/RTO/RPO, identity/PKI/network/observability/security/privacy controls, operating model, target regions, data-residency decisions, vendor environments/entitlements, support terms, actual quotes, and migration owners.

## Decision status

Approve the evidence-closure programme, not a platform selection. Kong hybrid variants remain low-confidence priority-validation hypotheses subject to the same E1/E2 screen as every exact variant. No weighted product scores or final product recommendation are published because mandatory-gate, organization-specific, comparative execution, TCO, risk, and support evidence is not yet complete.

Content publication is subject to the same discipline: no asset is promoted from brief or protocol to decision-grade study until its evidence, comparative treatment, inline figures, metadata, limitations, and independent review meet the applicable quality gate.
