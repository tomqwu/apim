## Content maturity boundary

The delivered portal, presentation routes, source manifest, visual rendering, validators and deep article corpus establish an **implemented repository/content baseline with independent acceptance open**. The [top-ten industry-problem study](../docs/43-api-management-industry-problems.md) provides a problem-led research portfolio; the [industry-practices study](../docs/45-api-management-industry-practices.md) turns it into operating patterns, eight synthetic practice cases, anti-patterns, evidence maturity and proof gates; the [Kong multicloud roadmap](../docs/44-kong-multicloud-study-roadmap.md) provides candidate-specific evidence sequencing; and the [Kong platform strategy](../docs/47-kong-enterprise-platform-strategy.md) records the stakeholder direction as one bounded self-managed target with measurable outcomes, a Konnect custody benchmark and a separate non-Kong exit. These artifacts do not establish an exact purchasable option, executed E3/E4 fit, support/TCO outcome or permission to scale. Product direction is not product evidence. Editorial closure is governed by the [principal content review](content-research-principal-review.md), its [remediation backlog](content-remediation-backlog.csv), and the [repository roadmap](../docs/39-repository-roadmap.md); no PCR item is closed without committed evidence and independent reviewer acceptance.

## Decision status

Record Kong as the stakeholder-selected planning direction and approve only the bounded, reversible `KP-SMH1` foundation and evidence programme. Self-managed hybrid is the leading custody model while control-plane/database sovereignty, multicloud runtime placement and management-loss continuity remain hard priorities; Konnect is the same-vendor operating benchmark when lifecycle reduction matters more. Exact option, E2 contract/support, E3 failure/recovery, E4 representative operation, staffing, TCO and true non-Kong exit evidence still govern production scale. No weighted comparative winner or achieved outcome is claimed.

Content publication is subject to the same discipline: no asset is promoted from brief or protocol to decision-grade study until its evidence, comparative treatment, inline figures, metadata, limitations, and independent review meet the applicable quality gate.

The publication workflow controls how an evidence delta is released; it does not raise that evidence's level. Its mutable run checkpoint remains outside the reviewed tree and records the candidate, independently accepted and CI-checked SHA, PR merge, Pages run, live manifest assertions and residual limitations without creating a self-referential commit.
