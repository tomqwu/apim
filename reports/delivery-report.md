## Content maturity boundary

The delivered portal, presentation routes, source manifest, visual rendering, validators and deep article corpus establish an **implemented repository/content baseline with independent acceptance open**. The [top-ten industry-problem study](../docs/43-api-management-industry-problems.md) provides a problem-led research portfolio; the [industry-practices study](../docs/45-api-management-industry-practices.md) turns it into operating patterns, eight synthetic practice cases, anti-patterns, evidence maturity and proof gates; and the [Kong multicloud roadmap](../docs/44-kong-multicloud-study-roadmap.md) provides one candidate-specific, falsifiable study sequence. None establishes exact purchasable options, organization fit, executed E3 comparisons, TCO/support outcomes, or a recommendation. Product symmetry in research design is not proof symmetry. Editorial closure is governed by the [principal content review](content-research-principal-review.md), its [remediation backlog](content-remediation-backlog.csv), and the [repository roadmap](../docs/39-repository-roadmap.md); no PCR item is closed without committed evidence and independent reviewer acceptance.

## Decision status

Approve Gate 0 and the prerequisite-driven evidence-closure programme, not a platform selection. Kong hybrid options remain named low-confidence sequencing hypotheses with no priority and are subject to the same E1/E2 screen and Gate-1 option resolution as every bounded archetype. No weighted product scores or final product recommendation are published because mandatory-gate, organization-specific, comparative execution, TCO, risk, and support evidence is not yet complete.

Content publication is subject to the same discipline: no asset is promoted from brief or protocol to decision-grade study until its evidence, comparative treatment, inline figures, metadata, limitations, and independent review meet the applicable quality gate.

The publication workflow controls how an evidence delta is released; it does not raise that evidence's level. Its mutable run checkpoint remains outside the reviewed tree and records the candidate, independently accepted and CI-checked SHA, PR merge, Pages run, live manifest assertions and residual limitations without creating a self-referential commit.
