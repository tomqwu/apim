# ADR-0002: Advance Kong as the leading hypothesis

- Status: proposed
- Date: 2026-08-17

## Decision

Take Konnect hybrid and self-managed Kong variants into the deepest PoC while preserving APIM and Apigee as serious benchmarks.

## Conditions

No product selection is made. Kong must pass security/residency, topology/plugin, failure, performance, operations/support, migration, and commercial gates.
